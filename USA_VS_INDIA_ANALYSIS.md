**Build: "Rudus for HVAC"**

Core features:
1. Upload HVAC drawings (PDF/DWG)
2. AI detects:
   - Ductwork (supply, return)
   - Diffusers, grilles, registers
   - Mechanical equipment (AHU, RTU, exhaust fans)
   - Zones and CFM requirements
3. Auto-generate material takeoff:
   - Linear feet of duct by size (6", 8", 10", etc.)
