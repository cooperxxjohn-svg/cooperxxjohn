## 🎯 DIRECT COMPETITORS (HVAC/Plumbing Space)

### 1. **Rebar** - ⚠️ BIGGEST THREAT

**Company Info:**
- **Founded:** October 2024 (only 16 months old!)
- **Funding:** [$14M Series A (March 2026)](https://news.crunchbase.com/real-estate-property-tech/ai-generated-hvac-quotes-rebar-seriesa/)
- **Investors:** Prudence (lead), Zero Infinity Partners, Founder Collective, Villain Capital, Optimist Ventures
- **Location:** New York
- **Traction:** 40 clients, **doubled ARR in 6 weeks** (Feb-March 2026)

**What They're Building:**
- **Target:** HVAC **suppliers** (not contractors - important distinction!)
- **Product:** AI operating system for commercial HVAC quote generation
- **Technology:** 
  - Proprietary computer vision models
  - Analyzes construction blueprints
  - Identifies, categorizes, counts all HVAC equipment
  - Produces bill of materials
  - Generates quotes automatically
- **Speed:** 60-70% faster than traditional methods
- **Impact:** Win rates increasing 2-3x for customers

**What They Extract:**
- Tens of thousands of data points from each project
- From blueprints AND specification books
- Full equipment list with categorization

**Expansion Plans:**
- Currently: HVAC suppliers only
- **Next:** Plumbing and electrical equipment suppliers
- Timeline: Actively expanding (2026)

**Why They're Dangerous:**
- ✅ Well-funded ($14M vs most seed rounds $1-3M)
- ✅ Fast growth (doubled ARR in 6 weeks)
- ✅ Proven product-market fit (40 paying clients)
- ✅ Expanding to plumbing/electrical (your target markets!)
- ✅ Strong investor backing (Founder Collective is top-tier)

**Your Differentiation Opportunity:**
- ❌ They target **suppliers** (B2B2B)
- ✅ You can target **contractors** directly (B2B)
- ❌ They focus on quoting only
- ✅ You can do takeoffs + estimating + bidding (fuller solution)
- ❌ They're supplier-side (less volume)
- ✅ You're contractor-side (800K+ contractors vs ~5K suppliers)
