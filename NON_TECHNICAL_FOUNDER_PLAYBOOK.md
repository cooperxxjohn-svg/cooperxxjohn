#### 1. You (Non-Technical CEO/Co-founder)

**Your Job (50+ hours/week):**
- **Vision & Strategy** (10%)
  - Define product roadmap
  - Make build/no-build decisions
  - Competitive intelligence

- **Customer Discovery & Sales** (40%)
  - Interview 100+ potential customers
  - Find first 10-20 pilot users
  - Close first 5-10 paying customers
  - Build relationships with contractors/homeowners

- **Fundraising** (20%)
  - Pitch investors
  - Network with VCs/angels
  - Apply to accelerators (YC, Sequoia Surge, etc.)

- **Recruiting** (20%)
  - Find technical co-founder
  - Hire first employees
  - Build advisor network

- **Operations** (10%)
  - Manage team
  - Budget/finance
  - Legal/compliance

**Skills you need to learn:**
- Basic tech literacy (understand what's possible/impossible)
- Customer discovery (read "The Mom Test")
- Pitching (practice 100 times)
- Recruiting (selling vision to top talent)

**Best Programs (₹10-50L for 5-10% equity):**

1. **Y Combinator** (yc.com/apply)

   - Best brand, best network
   - Hard to get in (1-2% acceptance)

2. **Sequoia Surge** (surge.sequoiacap.com)
   - $1-2M (~₹8-16 Cr) for 6-10%
   - India-focused
   - Moderate acceptance (~5%)

3. **Peak XV Spark** (peakxv.com/spark)
   - ₹50L-2 Cr for 8-12%
   - Pre-seed focused
   - India-specific

4. **Accel Atoms** (accel.com/atoms)
   - ₹1-3 Cr for 8-10%
   - India focus

5. **100X.VC** (100x.vc)
   - ₹25-50 lakh for 8-10%
   - First-time founders welcome
   - Easiest to get into (~10-15% acceptance)

6. **IAN Alpha Fund** (indianangelnetwork.com)
   - ₹25-50 lakh
   - Mentor-heavy

**How to apply:**
1. Apply 3 months before you need money
2. Show traction (even 10 pilot users helps)

4. Practice pitch 50 times

## PART 5: COMMON MISTAKES (AVOID THESE)

### Mistake #1: Trying to Hire Too Many People

**Wrong:** "I need 10 people to build this"
**Right:** "I need 1 great technical co-founder who can build v1"

**Why:** Cash burns fast, coordination overhead kills you

---

### Mistake #2: Paying Market Salaries Too Early

**Wrong:** Offer ₹30L/year to senior engineer
**Right:** Offer ₹60K/month + 5% equity to hungry junior

**Why:** ₹30L/year = your entire budget gone in 4 months

---

### Mistake #3: Hiring for "Potential Future Needs"

**Wrong:** Hire ML engineer, backend, frontend, designer, DevOps on Day 1
**Right:** Hire 1 full-stack person who can do all

**Why:** You need generalists early, specialists later

---

### Mistake #4: Not Validating Before Building

**Wrong:** "Let me raise money first, then talk to customers"
**Right:** "Let me talk to 100 customers, then raise money with proof"

**Why:** Investors want evidence you understand problem

---

### Mistake #5: Giving Up After 10 Rejections

**Wrong:** "10 angels said no, idea must be bad"
**Right:** "10 no's = I need to improve pitch, keep going"

**Why:** Average is 50-100 no's before 1 yes
