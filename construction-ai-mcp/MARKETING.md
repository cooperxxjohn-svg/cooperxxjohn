## Problem Statement

### The Pain
Contractors and estimators spend **3-4 hours** creating detailed takeoff estimates for every project. They must:
- Calculate square footage
- Deduct openings (doors, windows)
- Figure out material quantities
- Estimate labor hours by phase
- Apply waste factors, overhead, profit
- Format everything professionally for bidding

**One mistake = lost money.**

### The Market
- **5M+ construction contractors** in the US alone
- **$2 trillion** annual construction spending
- **Every project needs an estimate** before bidding
- **Manual estimating is the bottleneck**

### Current Solutions
- **Manual spreadsheets:** Time-consuming, error-prone
- **Professional software:** Expensive ($500+/month), steep learning curve
- **Generic AI:** Gives vague guesses, not professional breakdowns

---

## Solution

### Construction AI = Claude + Construction Knowledge

**One-line install** gives Claude access to professional estimating tools.

**Natural language input:**
```
Estimate drywall for a 20x15 office with 9ft ceilings,
one door, two windows. Level 4 commercial finish.
```

**Professional output** in 40 seconds:
- Material quantities (32 sheets, 60 lbs compound, 354 ft tape)
- Labor breakdown (22 hrs hanging, 6 hrs taping, 6 hrs finishing)
- Complete costs ($3,938 total, $4.45/sqft)

**Industry-accurate:**
- ASTM C840 finish levels
- RS Means 2026 labor rates
- GA-214 standards
- Real-world waste factors and overhead
