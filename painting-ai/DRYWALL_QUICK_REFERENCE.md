## Quick Formulas

### Sheets Needed (4' × 12' = 48 sqft)
```
sheets = ceiling((total_sqft - large_openings) / 48 × (1 + waste_factor))
```
**Waste factors:** 15% base, +5% high ceiling, +10% ceiling work, +5% complex

### Joint Compound (by finish level)
| Level | lbs per 100 sqft | Typical Use |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| 1 | 2.0 | Fire assemblies |
| 2 | 3.5 | Behind tile |
| 3 | 5.3 | Residential standard |
| 4 | 7.5 | Commercial flat paint |
| 5 | 9.5 | High-end skim coat |

### Labor Hours (per 100 sqft)
| Task | Hours | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Framing | 0.5-0.7 | Metal studs @ 16" OC |
| Hanging walls | 1.2 | Standard 8-9' ceilings |
| Hanging ceiling | 1.8 | Overhead work |
| Level 3 finishing | 1.8 | Residential standard |
| Level 4 finishing | 2.2 | Commercial quality |
| Level 5 finishing | 3.0 | High-end w/ skim coat |

## Finish Level Decision Guide

### Level 1 - Tape Only
- Fire-rated assemblies
- Plenums and attics
- Areas not for habitation
- **Cost multiplier:** 0.3×

### Level 2 - One Coat
- Behind tile or panels
- Garages (non-living)
- Industrial warehouses
- **Cost multiplier:** 0.5×

### Level 3 - Two Coats (STANDARD)
- Typical residential
- Texture finishes
- Most homes
- **Cost multiplier:** 1.0× (baseline)

### Level 4 - Three Coats
- Flat paint critical
- Commercial offices
- Retail spaces
- **Cost multiplier:** 1.2×

### Level 5 - Skim Coat
- High-end residential
- Critical lighting areas
- Premium finishes
- **Cost multiplier:** 1.6×

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Forgetting ceiling area** - Walls AND ceiling both get drywall
2. **Deducting small openings** - Only deduct if ≥10 sqft
3. **Using wrong finish level** - Level 3 is residential standard, not Level 4
4. **Underestimating waste** - Ceiling work adds 10%, high ceilings add 5%
5. **Wrong sheet size** - Verify 4×8, 4×10, or 4×12 before calculating
6. **Ignoring complexity** - Soffits, arches, curves add significant labor
7. **Using flat rate labor** - Different rates for hanging vs. finishing
8. **Forgetting corner bead** - Outside corners need metal/vinyl bead

## Calculator Usage

### Basic Estimate
```python
from drywall_calculator import DrywallCalculator

calc = DrywallCalculator(labor_rate=65.0)

room = {
    "name": "Bedroom",
    "length": 12.0,
    "width": 15.0,
    "height": 8.0,
    "openings": [
```

## Quick Estimating Method (Field Use)

### Step 1: Calculate Area
```
Wall area = perimeter × height
Ceiling area = length × width
Total = walls + ceiling
```

### Step 2: Deduct Large Openings
```
Deductions = sum of openings ≥ 10 sqft
Net area = total - deductions
```

### Step 3: Calculate Sheets
```
Base sheets = net area / 48
Add 25% waste (typical)
Round up to whole sheets
```

### Step 4: Estimate Labor
```
Level 3: ~0.40 hours per sheet
Level 4: ~0.50 hours per sheet
Level 5: ~0.65 hours per sheet
```

### Step 5: Price It
```
Materials: sheets × $12.50 + compound + tape
Labor: hours × $65/hr
Subtotal: materials + labor
Total: subtotal × 1.40 (overhead + profit)
```

**Example:** 12×15 room, 8' ceiling
```
Area: 54×8 + 12×15 = 432 + 180 = 612 sqft
Deductions: 1 door (21) = 21 sqft
Net: 591 sqft
Sheets: 591/48 = 12.3 × 1.25 = 15 sheets
Labor: 15 × 0.40 = 6 hours (hanging + finishing ~18-20 total)
Materials: 15 × $12.50 + $50 = ~$240
Labor: 20 × $65 = $1,300
Subtotal: $1,540
Total: $1,540 × 1.40 = ~$2,150
```
