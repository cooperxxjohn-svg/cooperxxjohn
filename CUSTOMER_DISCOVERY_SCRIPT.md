# Customer Discovery Script - No Frontend Needed

**Goal:** Validate the problem and get 5-10 contractors to commit to using it when ready

## The Approach (No Frontend Required)

You don't need a UI to validate. You just need to prove you can solve their problem.

**What you CAN show:**
- ✅ Demo output (JSON with 127 line items)
- ✅ Sample Excel export (we can generate this)
- ✅ Cost breakdown
- ✅ "This is what you'll get in 40 seconds vs. 4 hours manually"

**What you DON'T show:**
- ❌ The upload interface (doesn't exist yet)
- ❌ The web app (not built)

## The Demo Call (15 Minutes)

### Part 1: Qualify (2 min)

**Questions to ask:**
1. "How many drywall estimates do you do per month?"
   - Need: 5+ per month (good customer)
   - Red flag: <2 per month (not worth it)

2. "How do you create estimates today?"
   - Manual Excel? → You save them 3-4 hours
   - PlanSwift? → You're 10x faster
   - Subcontractor bids? → Different use case

3. "What's the most time-consuming part?"
   - Counting walls? → AI helps
   - Calculating materials? → Assembly expansion helps
   - Pricing? → We have that

### Part 2: Show the Problem (2 min)

**Share screen: Show a floor plan**
- "This is a typical office renovation, 2,900 sqft"
- "How long would it take you to estimate this?"
- Expected answer: "3-4 hours"
- "What if I told you AI can do it in 40 seconds?"

### Part 3: Show the Solution (5 min)

**Share screen: Open demo_estimate_output.json**

**Walk through:**
1. "Here's what the AI detected automatically:"
   - 12 walls with dimensions
   - 3 ceilings
   - All openings (doors, windows)

2. "Here are the 127 detailed line items:"
   - Scroll through: Framing, drywall, compound, labor
   - "See? 2x4x10 studs: 158 each. You can order this directly."

3. "Here's the cost breakdown:"
   - Materials: $2,820
   - Labor by phase: $3,308
   - Total: $8,273 ($2.84/sqft)
   - "Does this pricing look reasonable for your market?"

4. "This exports to Excel, ready to send to clients"

**The key question:**
"If this took 40 seconds instead of 4 hours, would you use it?"

### Part 4: Objection Handling (3 min)

**Common objections:**

1. **"AI can't be accurate"**
   - "You're right to be skeptical. That's why you can review and edit everything."
   - "The AI detects, you verify, then export. Still saves you 3 hours."

2. **"I have my own pricing"**
   - "Perfect! You can customize all the unit costs."
   - "We just give you the quantities. You adjust the pricing to your rates."

3. **"I don't trust the measurements"**
   - "Fair. The AI gets it 90% right, you review the other 10%."
   - "Even if you spend 30 minutes reviewing, that's still 3.5 hours saved."

4. **"How much does it cost?"**
   - "Not finalized yet, but thinking $149/month for unlimited projects."
   - "If you do 10 estimates per month, that's $15 per estimate vs. $200 in your time."

5. **"Can I try it?"**
   - "It's not live yet - building the interface now."
   - "But if you send me a floor plan, I can run it through and show you the output."
   - "Would that be helpful?"

### Part 5: Close (3 min)

**The ask:**
"Here's where I need help. I'm building this and want to make sure it actually solves your problem."

**Three options:**

**Option A: Early access (Best)**
- "I'm launching in 4-6 weeks. Want early access?"
- "I'll set you up for free the first month to test it."
- "All I ask is 30 minutes of feedback after you use it."

**Option B: Send me a floor plan (Good)**
- "Send me one of your recent floor plans (PDF)."
- "I'll run it through the AI and send you the full estimate."
- "You can see exactly what it produces."

**Option C: Stay in touch (Okay)**
- "Can I email you when it's ready to try?"
- "I'll send you a demo video."

**Collect:**
- [ ] Email address
- [ ] Phone number (if enthusiastic)
- [ ] Commitment level (A, B, or C)
